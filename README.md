# ABC Communications Customer Churn Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn for ABC Communications Ltd to identify
customer segments and factors associated with customer attrition.

The project uses business analytics techniques to transform customer data
into meaningful insights and actionable recommendations that can support
customer retention and business decision-making.

Business Problem

ABC Communications Ltd wants to better understand why customers leave and
which customer segments are most vulnerable to churn.

The analysis investigates customer churn across key factors including:

- Contract type
- Customer tenure
- Internet service
- Payment method
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Customer service-related variables

Business Questions

The analysis seeks to answer the following questions:

1. What proportion of customers are churning?
2. Which contract types have the highest customer churn?
3. How does customer tenure relate to churn?
4. Which internet service customers experience the highest churn?
5. Which payment methods are associated with higher churn?
6. How do monthly charges differ between customers who stayed and those who
   churned?
7. What actions can ABC Communications take to reduce customer churn?

Key Findings

Overall Customer Churn

The analysis identified an overall customer churn rate of approximately
**26.54%**.

Customer Tenure

Customers with shorter tenure demonstrated substantially higher churn.

The churn rate was approximately:

- **48.28%** for customers with 0–11 months of tenure
- **29.51%** for customers with 12–23 months
- **22.03%** for customers with 24–35 months
- **19.52%** for customers with 36–47 months
- **15.00%** for customers with 48–59 months
- **6.68%** for customers with 60–72 months

This indicates that newer customers represent an important retention
priority.

Contract Type

Month-to-month customers represented approximately **55.02% of the customer
base** and showed substantially higher churn than customers on longer-term
contracts.

Internet Service

Fiber optic customers recorded approximately **42% churn**, compared with
approximately **19% for DSL customers**.

Payment Method

Customers using electronic checks recorded approximately **45% churn**, which
was considerably higher than the other payment methods analysed.

Monthly Charges

Customers who churned had average monthly charges of approximately **R76.28**,
compared with approximately **R60.81** among customers who stayed.

---

Business Risks

The analysis identified several business risks:

1. High churn among month-to-month customers may increase customer replacement
   and acquisition costs.
2. High churn among newer customers may indicate weaknesses in early customer
   engagement and onboarding.
3. High churn in specific service and payment segments may indicate potential
   customer experience, pricing or service-related concerns.

---

Business Opportunities

The analysis presents opportunities to:

- Improve early-tenure customer engagement.
- Encourage customers to move from month-to-month to longer-term contracts.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns.
- Investigate customer experience among high-churn service segments.
- Use customer analytics to identify customers at higher risk of churn.

---

Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. Develop targeted retention campaigns for month-to-month customers.
2. Introduce incentives that encourage customers to adopt longer-term
   contracts.
3. Implement an early-tenure customer engagement programme.
4. Investigate the causes of high churn among fiber optic customers.
5. Investigate the reasons for high churn among electronic-check customers.
6. Review pricing and value communication for customers with higher monthly
   charges.
7. Establish ongoing churn monitoring using customer analytics and
   dashboards.
